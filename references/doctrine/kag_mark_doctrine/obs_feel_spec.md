# Obs Feel Spec — What the Meta-RL Clone Must Feel

Not textbook features. Internal probabilities and attention must track these **relational predicates**. Perception failure = missed bread-and-butter while pattern present.

## Must distinguish from observation vector

1. **HTF force side** on both support TFs per official set (not average of all TFs).
2. **LTF velocity phase** relative to that force (with / against / flat).
3. **Inertia intact vs broken** (slow period still with tide during micro dip).
4. **Tunnel membership**: full-body outside both rails vs inside (macro vs micro).
5. **Topology class**: slingshot_load | release | launch | collapse | chop.
6. **Multi-set consensus** vs incomplete alignment.
7. **Continuation vs reversal** states (reversal legal when full alignment supports).
8. **Volatility / efficiency regime**: nothing / tradable / great movement; E→0 masks fires.
9. **Heat / floor / goal distance** (finish-line judgment, not only stop math).
10. **Wait subtype**: loaded-not-yet vs no-trade vs kill.

## Bread-and-butter target

Pay and teach: **pullback on LTF while both HTF supports stay strongly trending** → wait loaded → fire on resume with tide.

If policy sits flat on a clear bread-and-butter day → classify **Perception** (or Policy fear) under IRAC — not “no edge that day.”
